OBP Cohort

Run startup programmes, from first application to graduation.

Cohort gives incubators, accelerators, banks, regulators and innovation hubs one place for calls, applications, reviews, milestones and graduation - in English and Arabic, on your own infrastructure, built on the Open Bank Project.

Open calls, with Opey answering questions

The whole journey, in one place

  1. CallPublish a call on a public site. Anyone can find it and apply, no staff in the way.
  2. ApplyForms with attachments, save and resume, and eligibility checked on submission.
  3. EvaluateReviewers, rubrics, conflict-of-interest declarations and a ranked shortlist.
  4. DeliverMilestones, sessions, attendance, surveys and discussions for each startup.
  5. GraduateStage gates checked on the server, and a certificate in the startup's language.

For everyone in the programme

Startups

Programme staff

  • See who is behind, at a glance

Reviewers and mentors

  • Run sessions and record attendance

A closer look

Evaluation

Fair reviews, ranked

Each call has its own weighted rubric. Reviewers declare any conflict of interest before they see an application, then score it. Staff see the applications ranked by average score and record the decision, with who, when and why kept for the audit trail.

Dashboard

Know who is falling behind

Every call's funnel, from started to accepted, and every programme's startups by stage, milestones accepted and sessions attended. Startups that are behind or flagged stand out. Both tables download as CSV or PDF.

Startup space

Everything a startup needs

Announcements, its stage, its milestones and the sessions it has booked, on one page. Missed deadlines trigger reminders automatically, and graduation brings a certificate to print or save as PDF.

Arabic and English

Right to left, throughout

Every page works in Arabic and English, with the whole layout mirrored for right-to-left. Emails go out in both languages, and certificates in the startup's own.

Built on the Open Bank Project

Cohort has no database of its own. Every record lives in OBP-API, so it inherits a platform that banks already trust.

  • Role-based accessFine-grained Roles decide who sees and changes what; staff act with their own Roles
  • Audit trailDecisions, reviews and stage changes are append-only, with who, when and why
  • Safe uploadsFiles are quarantined until scanned, and staff never open one by surprise
  • Opey, the AI assistantAnswers applicants' questions about the calls on the page, with every conversation logged
  • Your brandName, logos and theme are configuration, changed live without a release
  • On your infrastructureRuns on-premises or in your cloud, with no external SaaS
  • Data retentionRecords are archived or deleted by policy, with approval first
  • Open sourcePart of OBP-Frontend on GitHub, AGPL licensed
